Base solution

Compute first, guess last. A router sends every question to the cheapest tool that can answer it exactly. The big model only steps in when nothing else works.

★ Innovation pick

Two witnesses

Every number is found two independent ways, e.g. geometry + vision, IFC + rendered PDF, or Gemini + Nemotron on a scan. Both agree → green. They differ → show both sets of marks, the human decides with one click.
